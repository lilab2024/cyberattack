# cyberattack
## Overview
Constructed six types of cyberattack scenarios, most of which are communication-based attacks, and systematically analyzed the response differences between ICE and EV under different attack conditions.
## Key Contributions

- **Cyberattack Modeling**  
  Six novel cyberattack types are systematically constructed, with a primary focus on communication-based attacks.

- **Simulation Framework**  
  Experiments are conducted in a ring-road simulation environment with:
  - Varying ACC Market Penetration Rates (MPRs)
  - Different spatial distributions of compromised vehicles

- **Risk-Based Classification**  
  A three-tier risk taxonomy is proposed to map disturbance metrics to actionable defense priorities.

## Findings

- EV platoons demonstrate:
  - Lower velocity standard deviation  
  - Reduced spacing oscillations  
  - Faster recovery after attacks  

- Compared to ICE vehicles, EVs exhibit an inherent advantage in traffic stability and cyber-resilience across all tested scenarios.
